# 📖  Chapter 6 — Compliance, Privacy & Microsoft Purview

---

## 🔹 1. Compliance

### 📖 Definition

Compliance is the process of following laws, regulations, standards, and organizational policies.

### 🎯 Purpose

To ensure an organization follows required legal, regulatory, and internal rules.

### 🌍 Example

A company follows GDPR requirements when handling customers' personal data.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Compliance means following the laws, regulations, and organizational policies that apply to an organization. It helps businesses operate legally, protect information, and meet required security and privacy standards.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Compliance = Following Rules**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Follow laws and regulations
* Follow organizational policies
* Helps avoid legal and regulatory problems
* Protects organizations and customers

---

## 🔹 2. Governance

### 📖 Definition

Governance is the process of managing and controlling IT resources according to business goals, policies, and requirements.

### 🎯 Purpose

To ensure technology, data, and resources are managed properly and support organizational objectives.

### 🌍 Example

A company creates rules defining which employees are allowed to access confidential customer data.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Governance is the process of establishing rules, policies, and controls to manage IT resources effectively. It ensures that technology and data are used responsibly and according to business requirements.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Governance = Managing Resources Properly**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Sets rules and policies
* Controls IT resources
* Supports business goals
* Helps manage risk

---

## 🔹 3. Privacy

### 📖 Definition

Privacy is the protection of personal information and control over how that information is collected, used, stored, and shared.

### 🎯 Purpose

To protect individuals' personal information and ensure it is handled appropriately.

### 🌍 Example

A website asks for permission before collecting a user's personal information.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Privacy focuses on how personal information is collected, used, stored, and shared. Organizations must handle personal data responsibly and respect individuals' privacy rights.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Privacy = Protect Personal Data**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Protects personal information
* Controls data collection and use
* Controls how information is shared
* Important for individuals and organizations

---

## 🔹 4. Microsoft Purview

### 📖 Definition

Microsoft Purview is Microsoft's platform for data governance, compliance, risk management, and information protection.

### 🎯 Purpose

To help organizations discover, classify, protect, manage, and govern their data.

### 🌍 Example

An organization uses Microsoft Purview to identify confidential documents and apply protection policies to them.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Microsoft Purview is a unified platform that helps organizations manage data governance, compliance, and information protection. It provides tools for classifying, protecting, retaining, and managing organizational data.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Purview = Compliance + Data Governance + Protection**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Data governance
* Compliance
* Information protection
* Data classification
* Risk management

---

## 🔹 5. Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

### 📖 Definition

Data Loss Prevention (DLP) helps prevent sensitive information from being shared, transferred, or exposed inappropriately.

### 🎯 Purpose

To prevent accidental or intentional leakage of sensitive information.

### 🌍 Example

Microsoft Purview DLP can detect a credit card number in an email and prevent the employee from sending it outside the organization.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Data Loss Prevention helps organizations identify and protect sensitive information. It can detect sensitive data and apply rules to prevent it from being shared or exposed inappropriately.

### ⭐ Remember This

**DLP = Prevent Data Leakage**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Detects sensitive information
* Prevents inappropriate sharing
* Protects confidential data
* Helps prevent data leakage

---

## 🔹 6. Sensitivity Labels

### 📖 Definition

Sensitivity labels are labels used to classify and protect sensitive documents, emails, and other content.

### 🎯 Purpose

To identify how sensitive information is and apply appropriate protection.

### 🌍 Example

A company labels a financial report as **Highly Confidential** so that only authorized employees can access it.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Sensitivity labels classify information according to its sensitivity and can apply protection such as encryption and access restrictions. They help organizations protect information based on its importance.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Sensitivity Labels = Classify + Protect**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

Common examples:

* Public
* Internal
* Confidential
* Highly Confidential

---

## 🔹 7. Information Protection

### 📖 Definition

Information protection is the process of protecting sensitive information using classification, encryption, access controls, and other security measures.

### 🎯 Purpose

To prevent sensitive information from being accessed, modified, or shared by unauthorized people.

### 🌍 Example

A company applies a sensitivity label and encryption to a confidential financial document.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Information protection focuses on identifying sensitive information and applying controls such as sensitivity labels, encryption, and access restrictions. This helps protect data wherever it is stored or shared.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Information Protection = Secure Sensitive Data**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Classify information
* Apply sensitivity labels
* Use encryption
* Control access

---

## 🔹 8. Insider Risk Management

### 📖 Definition

Insider Risk Management identifies and helps investigate potential risks caused by users inside an organization.

### 🎯 Purpose

To detect potentially harmful activities involving employees, contractors, or other authorized users.

### 🌍 Example

An employee downloads a large number of confidential files shortly before leaving the organization, triggering an insider-risk investigation.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Insider Risk Management helps organizations identify potentially risky activities performed by people who already have authorized access. It can help detect unusual behavior and investigate possible security or data risks.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Insider Risk = Risk from Inside**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Monitors risky activities
* Focuses on authorized users
* Helps identify unusual behavior
* Can help detect data-related risks

---

## 🔹 9. eDiscovery

### 📖 Definition

eDiscovery is the process of identifying, collecting, reviewing, and exporting electronic information for legal or regulatory investigations.

### 🎯 Purpose

To find relevant electronic data when it is needed for legal or compliance purposes.

### 🌍 Example

A company searches employee emails and documents to provide information required during a legal case.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

eDiscovery helps organizations find and manage electronic information that may be relevant to legal or regulatory investigations. It can be used to search, collect, review, and export relevant data.

### ⭐ Remember This

**eDiscovery = Find Data for Legal Cases**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Search electronic data
* Collect relevant information
* Review data
* Export data when required
* Used for legal investigations

---

## 🔹 10. Audit

### 📖 Definition

Audit is the process of recording and reviewing user and administrator activities within an organization.

### 🎯 Purpose

To provide visibility into what actions were performed and by whom.

### 🌍 Example

An administrator checks audit information to determine who deleted an important file.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Auditing provides a record of activities performed by users and administrators. Organizations can use audit information to investigate incidents, track changes, and understand what happened in their environment.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Audit = Activity History**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Records activities
* Tracks user actions
* Tracks administrator actions
* Helps investigate incidents

---

## 🔹 11. Audit Logs

### 📖 Definition

Audit logs are detailed records of activities performed within a system or organization.

### 🎯 Purpose

To provide evidence and information about system and user activities.

### 🌍 Example

An audit log can show when a user logged in, accessed a file, or changed a permission.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Audit logs contain detailed records of activities performed in a system. They help administrators investigate incidents, monitor changes, and determine what actions occurred.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Audit Logs = Detailed Activity Records**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

Examples include:

* Login activity
* File access
* Permission changes
* Administrative actions

## 🔹 12. Retention Policies

### 📖 Definition

Retention policies determine how long data should be retained and what happens to it after the retention period.

### 🎯 Purpose

To ensure information is kept for the required period and managed according to organizational or legal requirements.

### 🌍 Example

An organization creates a policy requiring certain emails to be retained for seven years.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Retention policies define how long an organization should keep certain data. They help organizations meet legal, regulatory, and business requirements for retaining or eventually disposing of information.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Retention = Keep Data for a Set Time**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Defines retention period
* Helps meet compliance requirements
* Controls data lifecycle
* Data may be deleted after the required period

---

## 🔹 13. Records Management

### 📖 Definition

Records Management is the process of managing important records throughout their lifecycle.

### 🎯 Purpose

To ensure important business records are properly classified, retained, protected, and disposed of when appropriate.

### 🌍 Example

A company manages legal contracts as official records and prevents them from being accidentally deleted.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Records Management helps organizations manage important business records throughout their lifecycle. It ensures records are properly retained, protected, and eventually disposed of according to organizational and regulatory requirements.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Records Management = Manage Important Records**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Manage important records
* Retain records properly
* Protect records
* Manage records throughout their lifecycle

### 🎯 Purpose

Compliance Manager helps organizations assess their compliance posture and identify actions that can improve it. It provides assessments, improvement actions, and a compliance score to help organizations track progress.

## 🔹 15. Data Lifecycle Management

### 📖 Definition

Data Lifecycle Management is the process of managing data from its creation through storage, use, retention, archiving, and eventual deletion.

### 🎯 Purpose

To ensure data is managed appropriately throughout its entire lifecycle.

### 🌍 Example

A company stores active documents, archives old records, and eventually deletes information that is no longer required.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Data Lifecycle Management manages information throughout its entire lifecycle, from creation to deletion. It helps organizations retain useful information while properly managing data that is no longer needed.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Data Lifecycle = Create → Manage → Delete**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

**Create → Store → Use → Retain/Archive → Delete**

---

## 🔹 16. Encryption

### 📖 Definition

Encryption is the process of converting readable data into an unreadable form to protect it from unauthorized access.

### 🎯 Purpose

To protect the confidentiality of sensitive information.

### 🌍 Example

HTTPS encrypts information exchanged between your browser and a secure website.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Encryption protects information by converting readable data into an unreadable form. Authorized users or systems can decrypt the information when they have the required key.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Encryption = Protect Data from Unauthorized Reading**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Protects confidentiality
* Makes data unreadable
* Uses encryption keys
* Can protect data in transit and at rest

---

## 🔹 17. Data Classification

### 📖 Definition

Data Classification is the process of organizing information according to its sensitivity, importance, or business value.

### 🎯 Purpose

To determine the appropriate level of protection for different types of information.

### 🌍 Example

A company classifies documents as **Public, Internal, Confidential, or Highly Confidential**.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

Data classification organizes information based on its sensitivity and importance. Once data is classified, organizations can apply appropriate security controls and protection policies.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Classification = Organize by Sensitivity**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Identifies sensitive data
* Categorizes information
* Helps determine protection
* Works with sensitivity labels

---

## 🔹 18. Legal Hold

### 📖 Definition

A Legal Hold prevents relevant information from being deleted or disposed of when it may be required for a legal investigation or case.

### 🎯 Purpose

To preserve potentially relevant information during legal proceedings.

### 🌍 Example

During a lawsuit, a company places relevant emails and documents on legal hold so they are preserved for the case.

### 🎤 Interview Answer (20–30 sec)

A Legal Hold preserves information that may be relevant to a legal case or investigation. It prevents the relevant data from being automatically deleted while the legal matter is ongoing.

### ⭐ Remember This

**Legal Hold = Stop Deletion for Legal Reasons**

### ⚡ Quick Revision

* Preserves relevant data
* Used during legal cases
* Prevents deletion
* Supports eDiscovery and investigations
